# Privacy Policy & KVKK Disclosure for Dilekçemetre **Last Updated:** October 18, 2026 **Application Name:** Dilekçemetre **Package Name:** com.aistudio.dilekcemetre.dmtrpa **Data Controller / Developer:** Hakan Bayramoğlu **Contact Email:** hakan@bayramoglu.biz --- ## English Version (For Google Play & International Compliance) ### 1. Introduction This Privacy Policy describes how Dilekçemetre ("the App", "we", "us") handles user information. We respect your privacy and adhere strictly to the Turkish Law on the Protection of Personal Data No. 6698 (KVKK) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). ### 2. Zero-Cloud Architecture & On-Device Storage The App is built with an **on-device privacy-first architecture**: - **Personal Identification Data:** You may optionally enter your Full Name, Republic of Turkey Identification Number (T.C. Kimlik Numarası), Phone Number, and Address in the profile section to auto-fill petition templates. - **Strictly Local:** All of this personal information, as well as the drafts of your petitions, are stored **strictly and solely on your physical device** (using Android SharedPreferences and a local SQLite Room Database). - **No Developer Server:** We do NOT operate any remote server or cloud database. We never collect, transmit, view, or synchronize your T.C. Kimlik Number, name, contact details, or petition drafts. ### 3. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Features - When utilizing the optional "AI Petition Drafting" feature, the prompt description you type is transmitted to the **Google Gemini API** (Google LLC) via encrypted HTTPS/TLS to generate petition text. - **Safety Advisory:** Users are advised not to type sensitive personal identifiers (such as T.C. Kimlik No) directly into the AI prompt box. Petitions can be generated using placeholders and completed locally on your device. ### 4. Third-Party Advertising & Diagnostics (Google AdMob) The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob to provide free features and rewarded PDF exports. Google AdMob may process pseudonymous diagnostic metrics, including: - Google Advertising ID (GAID) - Coarse IP address (for general location and fraud protection) - Ad interaction statistics No personal profile information or petition texts are ever shared with advertising partners. You may reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android Settings: `Settings > Google > Ads`. ### 5. Data Deletion and User Rights Since all personal data is stored locally on your device: - You can clear your profile details anytime via the in-app Profile menu. - You can instantly and permanently erase all stored petitions and data by clearing the app data (`Android Settings > Apps > Dilekçemetre > Storage > Clear Data`) or by uninstalling the application. - For formal privacy inquiries, contact: `hakan@bayramoglu.biz`. --- ## Türkçe Bölüm (6698 Sayılı KVKK Uyarınca Aydınlatma Metni) **Veri Sorumlusu:** Hakan Bayramoğlu **Uygulama:** Dilekçemetre (`com.aistudio.dilekcemetre.dmtrpa`) **İletişim:** hakan@bayramoglu.biz İşbu Aydınlatma Metni, 6698 sayılı Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu’nun ("KVKK") 10. maddesi uyarınca, **Dilekçemetre** mobil uygulamasını kullanan kullanıcılarımızın aydınlatılması amacıyla hazırlanmıştır. ### 1.
